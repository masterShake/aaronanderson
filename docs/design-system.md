# Design system

_Skeleton from W0; the kit lane completes it._

- Tokens: `src/styles/tokens.css` (and `tokens.ts` for SVG and three). Frozen.
- Faces: Big Shoulders (display), Archivo (body and UI), Martian Mono (labels), Architects
  Daughter (hand notes), plus a small symbols face listed first in every stack.
- Components: `src/components/{chrome,ui,blueprint}`. Their props are frozen; the kit lane
  owns their bodies. The `/kit` page (preview only) shows them on one sheet.
